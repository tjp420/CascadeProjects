## 4. Demo Script for Sales Calls

### Opening (2 minutes)
**Objective**: Establish rapport, confirm audience role, set agenda

**Script**:
> "Thanks for joining. Before we dive in, I want to make sure we're addressing your biggest pain points. What are the top security challenges your team is facing right now? [Listen, take notes]
> 
> Today, I'd like to walk you through SimpleBeacon and show you how it could help. We'll spend about 20-25 minutes showing a real example, then I'm happy to answer questions. Sound good?"

### Problem Identification (3-5 minutes)
**Objective**: Connect their challenges to SimpleBeacon's value

**Script** (Customize based on response):
> "I hear that a lot. Many teams we talk to struggle with [their specific pain point]. The root cause is usually fragmented tools—security data is siloed, response is manual, and compliance reporting is a nightmare.
> 
> That's where SimpleBeacon comes in. We've designed it specifically to solve this."

### Solution Overview (2 minutes)
**Script**:
> "SimpleBeacon is a unified platform that brings together threat detection, automated response, and compliance in one place. Think of it as the 'single pane of glass' your team actually wants to use.
> 
> Here's what makes us different: [Show comparison slide or key differentiators]"

### Live Demo / Walkthrough (10-15 minutes)
**Demo Flow**:

1. **Dashboard Overview** (2 min)
   - Show real-time threat feeds
   - Highlight key metrics (threats detected, incidents responded, compliance status)
   - Point out customizable widgets

2. **Threat Detection in Action** (3-4 min)
   - Walk through a sample incident
   - Show how the platform detected the threat
   - Highlight detection speed and confidence score

3. **Workflow Automation** (3-4 min)
   - Show a saved playbook (e.g., "Phishing Email Response")
   - Demonstrate how it auto-triggers on threat detection
   - Show MTTR reduction (hours → minutes)

4. **Compliance Dashboard** (2-3 min)
   - Show SOC 2 or ISO 27001 compliance status
   - Demonstrate one-click report generation
   - Highlight audit trail and evidence collection

5. **Integrations** (optional, 2 min)
   - Briefly show connected tools (Slack, PagerDuty, Jira, SIEM, etc.)
   - Emphasize API-first design and extensibility

### Proof of Value (2-3 minutes)
**Script**:
> "Let me show you what customers typically see after 90 days with SimpleBeacon:
> - [X]% faster incident response
> - [X]% fewer false positives
> - [X] compliance audits passed without external consultants
> - [X]% team time saved on manual tasks
> 
> These numbers come from [industry/company type] like yours."

### Addressing Concerns (Variable, handle as they arise)

**Common Objections & Responses**:

| Concern | Response |
|---------|----------|
| "We already have a SIEM" | SimpleBeacon complements your SIEM—we integrate with it to add faster detection, automation, and compliance orchestration |
| "Integration is complex" | Our team handles onboarding; most integrations take 2-4 weeks. We can do a pilot in your lab first. |
| "What about data residency?" | We support multi-region deployments, including on-prem or hybrid. Full control over where data lives. |
| "How do you compare to [Competitor]?" | [Prepared comparison based on your differentiators]. Would you like a head-to-head demo? |
| "What's the cost?" | Pricing depends on your scale and needs. Starter plans begin at $[X]/month. Let's discuss your requirements, and I can provide a custom quote. |

### Next Steps (1-2 minutes)
**Script** (Choose path based on feedback):

**Path A (High Interest)**:
> "I can see this could really help your team. Here's what I'd suggest:
> 1. Let's schedule a quick follow-up with your [CTO/CISOs/ops team]
> 2. We'll set up a free 14-day pilot in your environment
> 3. You'll see real data from your network, not just demos
> 4. We'll reconvene in 2 weeks and discuss next steps
> 
> Does that timeline work?"

**Path B (Cautious Interest)**:
> "Thanks for your time today. I know you'll want to chat with your team. Here's what I'd recommend:
> 1. I'll send over a one-pager and some case studies
> 2. Let's catch up next week to see if there are any other questions
> 3. If it looks promising, we can talk about a pilot
> 
> Fair?"

**Path C (Not Ready)**:
> "I totally understand—security platform decisions take time. Let me leave you with:
> 1. Our one-pager and a few customer case studies
> 2. My contact info; reach out anytime
> 3. We'll check in quarterly to see if things have shifted
> 
> Sound good?"

### Closing
> "Thanks again for your time. Feel free to reach out with any questions, and I look forward to staying in touch."

## 6. Open Questions for Sales Leadership

- **Target ICP**: Which industries, company sizes, and use cases are we prioritizing?
- **ARR Thresholds**: What's our target annual revenue? Which tiers should we push?
- **Competitive Positioning**: Who are the top 3 competitors? How do we position against them?
- **Sales Process**: What's the ideal sales cycle (30/60/90 days)?
- **Case Study Availability**: Do we have customer wins we can reference?
- **Support & Training**: What level of pre-sale technical support do we provide?
- **Demo Environment**: Do we have a stable, realistic demo environment?
- **Discount Authority**: Who approves discounts, and what are approval limits?
- **Sales Compensation**: What's the commission structure and quota?
- **Marketing Alignment**: What leads/campaigns can marketing drive to support sales?
