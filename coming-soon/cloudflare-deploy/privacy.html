Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

What we collect

  • Account email and authentication metadata (hosted dashboard)
  • Billing information processed by Stripe (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Waitlist signups and support correspondence
  • Scan reports you upload or generate (stored per your deployment)

What we do not do by default

  • We do not mutate your source code during standard scans
  • We do not sell your repository contents
  • CLI-only usage can run entirely on your machine with no cloud upload

Retention

Hosted scan history and assessments follow configured retention (default assessment TTL 24h where enabled). You may request deletion of account data at privacy@simplebeacon.ai.

Cookies

Authentication sessions use HTTP-only cookies or bearer tokens on the dashboard. Landing pages use minimal client-side storage for pricing interval preference.
