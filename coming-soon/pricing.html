FAQ

What is the $19 Instant Report and how does zero-retention work?

The $19 Instant Code Hygiene Report runs a gate scan on your local repository for credentials, mock data, and AI imports. Pay via Stripe, run the scan in your browser sandbox, and download a PDF certificate within 60 seconds. Zero-retention means no source code ever leaves your machine. The scan runs entirely in your browser. We do not store, log, or transmit your source code. Ever.

What is the Free Risk Assessment and what do I get?

The complimentary Risk Assessment runs entirely on your machine using our open-source CLI. You get an A–F grade, a preliminary risk profile for compliance teams, a list of AI-generated slop and credential leaks, and a remediation checklist. No source code ever leaves your infrastructure. Use it to evaluate SimpleBeacon before purchasing an Executive Certificate.

How is the Executive Risk Certificate different from the Free Risk Assessment?

The Free Risk Assessment runs a lightweight hygiene sweep (gate scan). The $499 Executive Risk Certificate runs all 15 SimpleBeacon analysis engines — gate, consolidation, mock data, roadmap, codebase, file reduction, data quality, cleanup, npm audit, compliance, EU AI Act readiness, dependency vulns, build readiness, AI indicators, and governance. It exports a ZIP containing a printable PDF certificate with cryptographic SHA-256 seal, plus 15 per-module JSON files with full findings. Designed for auditor presentations, due diligence documentation, regulatory filings, and vendor handoff.

Do I need a SaaS subscription to use these deliverables?

No. The $19 Instant Report and $2,499 EU AI Act Sprint are self-service — download your PDF instantly from certificate-upload.html. Other board deliverables are one-time engagements — you run the scan locally, export anonymized JSON, and download your signed certificate within 48 hours. No recurring SaaS fees, no seat licenses, no feature gates.

How does the EU AI Act Sprint self-service workflow work?

After payment, you receive a license token and dashboard URL instantly. The token unlocks the EU AI Act scanner in your dashboard where you can either upload your source code zip or select a local directory. The scan runs on your machine (no code leaves your infrastructure) and generates an executive PDF immediately. Download your report from certificate-upload.html — no operator review required.

Is the EU AI Act Sprint a legal conformity certification?

No. It is a standardized technical attestation — an automated cryptographic snapshot proving that as of the scan timestamp, local static-analysis detected specific compliance indicators inside the repository boundary. It scans your codebase for Annex III high-risk AI patterns, Article 50 transparency gaps, and documentation completeness. It produces an executive PDF with findings and a 30-day remediation plan. It does not make your company legally immune. For full legal conformity certification, engage a qualified EU legal firm.

How long are license tokens valid?

License tokens expire based on your tier:

Unlimited — Free AI Slop Audit (open-source CLI, no expiry)
7 days — $19 Instant Report
90 days — Executive Risk Certificate ($499)
30 days — EU AI Act Sprint ($2,499) & Continuous Shield

Paid tokens unlock the dashboard immediately after purchase. You can run unlimited scans during the validity period. After expiry, simply purchase a new token or contact us for a Continuous Shield subscription with auto-renewal.

What is your refund policy?

License tokens are digital keys consumed immediately upon issuance. For self-service tiers ($19, $499), we offer a 48-hour satisfaction window — if the scanner fails to process your repository or the deliverable is materially defective, contact us for a replacement token or refund. Enterprise contracts ($2,499+) include a 14-day review period with direct analyst support. See our refund policy for complete terms.

What data does SimpleBeacon transmit during a scan?

Deterministic scan: Zero transmission. The scan runs entirely on your machine reading local files. No source code, file paths, or credentials are uploaded.

Optional AI summary: Only anonymized scan statistics (issue counts, gate pass/fail, redacted path labels like "…/my-project/src") are sent to your chosen AI provider. Full file paths and source code are never included.

Can I pay by invoice or ACH instead of Stripe?

For orders over $5,000 or enterprise contracts, we accept invoice, ACH, and wire transfer. Contact us for a tailored proposal and custom billing terms.
