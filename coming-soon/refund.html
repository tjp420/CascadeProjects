Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Free Tier

The free CLI and MCP tools are provided at no cost. No refunds are applicable to the free tier.

2. Executive Clearance PDF ($499)

Refunds for executive clearance PDFs are handled on a case-by-case basis. To request a refund:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • Explain the issue with the report
  • We will review and respond within 3 business days

Refunds may be issued if:

  • The report contains significant errors
  • The report does not match the gate output you provided
  • Technical issues prevented delivery

Refunds will not be issued if:

  • You simply changed your mind
  • The report accurately reflects your gate output
  • You did not run the gate locally before requesting the PDF

3. Agency Packs ($999+)

Agency packs include milestone scans and white-label certificates. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis based on:

  • How much of the service has been delivered
  • The nature of any issues encountered
  • Timeline of the refund request

4. Cloud Teams ($49/mo)

Cloud Teams is a subscription service. You may cancel at any time. Refunds for partial months are not provided, but you will retain access until the end of your current billing period.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us with:

  • Your order details
  • The reason for your refund request
  • Any relevant documentation

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Stripe and may take 5-10 business days to appear in your account, depending on your bank's processing times.

7. Exceptions

We reserve the right to deny refund requests in cases of abuse, fraud, or violation of our terms of service.

8. Contact

For refund-related questions, please contact us.
