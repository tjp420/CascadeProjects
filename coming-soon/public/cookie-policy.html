Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Our No-Tracking Commitment

SimpleBeacon is engineered with a strict zero-tracking architecture. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling. Our core product runs entirely offline on your local machine — no source code, file paths, or repository contents are ever transmitted.

2. Cookies We Do Not Use

We explicitly do not use any of the following:

  • Advertising cookies (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Behavioral profiling or fingerprinting cookies
  • Third-party social media tracking cookies
  • Remarketing or retargeting cookies

3. Cookies and Storage We Use

We use only the minimum storage necessary for authentication and core functionality:

  • Authentication tokens — JWT tokens stored in localStorage under keys sb_token, cascadeAuthToken, or simplebeacon_token. These are set when you sign in and removed when you sign out. They allow access to the dashboard, audit history, and certificate downloads.
  • User profile — Your tier and role stored in localStorage under sb_user or sb-user. Used to display the correct navigation (e.g., hiding pricing for paid users).
  • Session cookies — SameSite=Lax cookies used solely for authentication propagation between pages. These are cleared on sign-out via max-age=0.

4. Analytics (Cookie-Free)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. It aggregates anonymous, non-identifying traffic metrics (page views, country-level location). No cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting, and no personal data is shared with third parties.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is loaded only on the production origin (simplebeacon.ai) and is automatically skipped in Brave and other tracker-blocking browsers to avoid SRI console errors.

5. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may set their own cookies when you interact with their embedded functionality. We do not control these cookies — each service is subject to its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe — Payment processing. Stripe may set cookies on its checkout pages for fraud prevention. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Font delivery. Google may receive a request for the font stylesheet. No cookies are set by Google Fonts itself.
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DDoS protection, and privacy-friendly analytics. Cloudflare's analytics beacon does not set cookies.

6. How to Disable or Delete Cookies

You can control cookies and local storage at any time:

  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via Settings → Privacy → Cookies. See your browser's help documentation.
  • Sign out — Signing out of SimpleBeacon clears your authentication tokens and session cookies immediately.
  • Clear local storage — In your browser's developer tools (F12 → Application → Local Storage → simplebeacon.ai), you can manually delete sb_token, sb_user, and related keys.
  • Private/incognito mode — Using private browsing mode ensures all storage is cleared when you close the window.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under the GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Know what cookies and storage we use (this policy)
  • Refuse non-essential cookies (we only use essential authentication storage)
  • Request deletion of all stored data (sign out + clear local storage, or email admin@simplebeacon.ai)
  • Opt out of analytics (use a tracker-blocking browser or extension — our analytics already skip Brave)

Because we do not use advertising or tracking cookies, no cookie consent banner is required. Our use of local storage is strictly for authentication, which falls under the "strictly necessary" exemption.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy to reflect changes in our services or regulatory requirements. Enterprise clients will be notified of significant changes via email at least 30 days before they take effect.

9. Contact

For cookie-related questions or data deletion requests, please contact us or email admin@simplebeacon.ai.
