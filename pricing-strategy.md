# Pricing Strategy - SimpleBeacon 2.0

## Overview
This document defines the pricing tiers, billing models, and trial policies for SimpleBeacon 2.0 launch.

## Pricing Tiers

### Developer Plan
- **Monthly:** $49/month
- **Annual:** $490/year (16.7% discount vs monthly)
- **Target Users:** Individual developers, small projects, proof-of-concept work
- **Features:**
  - Basic vulnerability scanning
  - Up to 10 projects
  - Community support
  - API access (rate-limited)
  - Monthly reports

### Team Pro Plan
- **Monthly:** $149/month
- **Annual:** $1,490/year (16.7% discount vs monthly)
- **Target Users:** Development teams, growing organizations
- **Features:**
  - Advanced vulnerability scanning
  - Up to 50 projects
  - Email support (24 business hours)
  - Full API access
  - Weekly automated reports
  - Team collaboration tools
  - Single sign-on (SSO)

### Legacy Pro Plan
- **Monthly:** $9/month (legacy pricing - grandfathered)
- **Annual:** $90/year
- **Status:** Deprecated tier for existing customers
- **Migration:** Existing Legacy Pro customers encouraged to upgrade to Team Pro with 30-day notice

### Enterprise Plan
- **Pricing:** Custom, quoted per engagement
- **Sales Process:** Direct sales, dedicated support
- **Target Users:** Large organizations, mission-critical deployments
- **Features:**
  - Unlimited projects and scanning
  - Dedicated account manager
  - 24/7 priority support
  - Custom integrations
  - Advanced security features
  - Compliance certifications
  - SLA guarantees

## Trial and Onboarding

### Free Trial
- **Duration:** 14 days
- **Plan Level:** Team Pro features (full access)
- **Requirements:** Valid email address, no payment method required
- **Conversion:** Auto-converts to Team Pro paid plan at trial end
- **Auto-renewal:** Optional subscription setup available 48 hours before trial ends

### Upgrade/Downgrade
- **Timing:** Effective immediately or at period end (user choice)
- **Proration:** Pro-rated charges/credits applied
- **No Lock-in:** Cancel anytime, no long-term contracts

## Payment Terms

### Monthly Billing
- Charged on the same day each month
- Access continues until explicitly cancelled
- Refunds not available for unused portions

### Annual Billing
- Billed upfront for 12 months
- 16.7% discount compared to monthly rate
- Auto-renews annually unless cancelled 30 days before renewal
- Pro-rated refund if cancelled within first 30 days

## Discount Schedule

| Billing Cycle | Developer | Team Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $49 | $149 | Custom |
| Annual | $490 (−16.7%) | $1,490 (−16.7%) | Custom |

### Volume Discounts
- Organizations with 5+ seats: Contact sales
- Annual commitment: Minimum 20% discount (reviewed case-by-case)

## FAQ

### Q: Can I switch between plans?
**A:** Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately or at the start of your next billing cycle, your choice.

### Q: What happens when my trial expires?
**A:** Your trial will end, and you'll need to enter a payment method to continue using SimpleBeacon. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends.

### Q: Do you offer refunds?
**A:** We do not offer refunds for unused time on paid subscriptions. However, if you cancel within 30 days of an annual prepayment, we can process a pro-rated refund.

### Q: Is there a discount for annual billing?
**A:** Yes! Annual billing is 16.7% cheaper than paying monthly. For example, Developer plan is $490/year instead of $588/year monthly.

### Q: Can I use SimpleBeacon on multiple devices/computers?
**A:** Yes. Your account is tied to your email, so you can log in from any device. Team Pro includes up to 5 team members on the same account.

### Q: Do you offer Enterprise licensing?
**A:** Yes. If you need unlimited seats, advanced security, or custom terms, please contact our sales team at sales@simplebeacon.io.

### Q: What happens if I exceed my project limit?
**A:** Developer plan allows 10 projects. If you need more, upgrade to Team Pro (50 projects) or contact sales for Enterprise options.

### Q: Is there a free tier?
**A:** We don't offer a permanent free plan, but all new users get a 14-day free trial of Team Pro features with no payment method required.
