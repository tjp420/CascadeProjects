# ReasonAI Brand Guidelines

## Logo & Identity

### Primary Logo
- **Text**: "ReasonAI" in custom typography
- **Colors**: Primary Blue (#3b82f6) gradient
- **Tagline**: "I don't just give answers - I teach you how to reason"

### Brand Colors
- **Primary Blue**: #3b82f6 (RGB: 59, 130, 246)
- **Primary Dark**: #1e40af (RGB: 30, 64, 175)
- **Secondary Gray**: #64748b (RGB: 100, 116, 139)
- **Accent Red**: #ef4444 (RGB: 239, 68, 68)
- **Background Light**: #f8fafc (RGB: 248, 250, 252)

### Typography
- **Headings**: Inter, Bold
- **Body**: Inter, Regular
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono

## Brand Voice

### Personality
- **Educational**: Clear, instructional, patient
- **Intelligent**: Precise, logical, stepwise
- **Approachable**: Friendly, supportive, encouraging
- **Professional**: Reliable, trustworthy, expert

### Key Messaging
- Focus on "teaching how to reason" vs "giving answers"
- Emphasize stepwise process and understanding
- Highlight confidence and learning outcomes
- Position as accessible, affordable education

## Visual Elements

### Iconography
- Clean, modern line icons
- Consistent stroke width (1.5px)
- Primary blue color scheme

### Layout Principles
- Clean, minimal design
- Ample white space
- Clear hierarchy
- Mobile-first responsive

## Usage Guidelines

### Do's
- Use full logo with tagline for primary branding
- Maintain consistent color palette
- Keep typography clean and readable
- Focus on educational value proposition

### Don'ts
- Don't distort or alter logo proportions
- Don't use unapproved colors
- Don't overcrowd layouts
- Don't compromise readability

## Domain & Digital Presence

## Marketing Materials

### Website Copy Examples
- "Master problem-solving with stepwise AI explanations"
- "Learn the logic behind every answer"
- "From confusion to clarity, one step at a time"

### Email Templates
- Welcome: Focus on learning journey
- Onboarding: Highlight stepwise reasoning benefits
- Progress: Celebrate learning milestones

## Competitive Positioning

### Differentiators
1. **Stepwise Focus**: Only AI that teaches reasoning process
2. **Educational Design**: Built for learning outcomes
3. **Affordable Access**: Lower cost than traditional tutoring
4. **Privacy-First**: Local processing, data protection

### Value Proposition
- **For Students**: Better grades through deeper understanding
- **For Parents**: Affordable educational support
- **For Educators**: Teaching assistant for complex concepts
- **For Professionals**: Skill development with clear progression
