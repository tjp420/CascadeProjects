# Outreach Campaign Launch Plan

## Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name**: "Automate Your Code Quality" 
**Launch Date**: Week 1 of go-to-market execution
**Target**: 50 mid-market engineering leaders
**Goal**: 15 demo bookings in first 4 weeks

## Week 1: Campaign Launch

### **Day 1: Monday - Initial Outreach**
**Target**: Top 20 prospects (highest priority)
**Action**: Send personalized outreach emails
**Template**: Primary outreach template with company-specific customization
**Time**: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM (local time zones)

#### **Email Batch 1 (9:00 AM - 10:30 AM)**
- **Prospects 1-10**: E-commerce companies
- **Focus**: Performance optimization and conversion impact
- **Personalization**: Recent product launches, scalability challenges

#### **Email Batch 2 (10:30 AM - 12:00 PM)**
- **Prospects 11-20**: FinTech companies  
- **Focus**: Security compliance and regulatory requirements
- **Personalization**: Recent funding rounds, compliance announcements

### **Day 2: Tuesday - LinkedIn Connections**
**Target**: All 50 prospects
**Action**: Send LinkedIn connection requests
**Template**: Personalized connection message
**Time**: 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM

#### **Connection Strategy**
- **Personalized Note**: Reference company achievement or recent news
- **Value Proposition**: Brief mention of automated remediation benefits
- **Follow-up Plan**: Message after connection acceptance

### **Day 3: Wednesday - Follow-up #1**
**Target**: Prospects who opened emails (tracked via email platform)
**Action**: Send technical whitepaper
**Template**: Whitepaper follow-up with ROI focus
**Time**: 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM

#### **Whitepaper Distribution**
- **Subject**: "Why Automated Remediation is the Future of Code Quality"
- **Content**: 10-page technical whitepaper
- **Call-to-Action**: Schedule 15-minute technical discussion

### **Day 4: Thursday - LinkedIn Engagement**
**Target**: Accepted LinkedIn connections
**Action**: Send personalized LinkedIn messages
**Template**: Value-focused message with demo offer
**Time**: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

#### **LinkedIn Message Strategy**
- **Reference**: Common connection or shared interest
- **Value**: Specific benefit for their company
- **Next Step**: Offer to share relevant case study

### **Day 5: Friday - Follow-up #2**
**Target**: Prospects who engaged with whitepaper
**Action**: Send case study follow-up
**Template**: Case study with similar company profile
**Time**: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

#### **Case Study Selection**
- **E-commerce**: Fashion Nova success story
- **FinTech**: Stripe security automation case
- **SaaS**: Asana feature velocity improvement

## Week 2: Campaign Expansion

### **Day 8: Monday - ROI Calculator Push**
**Target**: All prospects who haven't responded
**Action**: Send ROI calculator link
**Template**: ROI-focused email with calculator
**Time**: 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM

#### **ROI Calculator Strategy**
- **Personalized Inputs**: Pre-filled with company data
- **Immediate Value**: Shows potential savings
- **Next Step**: Offer to refine with actual data

### **Day 9: Tuesday - Demo Invitations**
**Target**: Warm prospects (engaged with content)
**Action**: Send personalized demo invitations
**Template**: Demo invitation with specific benefits
**Time**: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

#### **Demo Invitation Strategy**
- **Personalization**: Reference their specific pain points
- **Value**: Show how we solve their exact problems
- **Logistics**: Offer multiple time options

### **Day 10: Wednesday - Social Proof**
**Target**: Prospects considering demo
**Action**: Share customer testimonials
**Template**: Social proof email with testimonials
**Time**: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

#### **Social Proof Strategy**
- **Video Testimonials**: From similar companies
- **Written Quotes**: From engineering leaders
- **Metrics**: Concrete results and ROI

### **Day 11: Thursday - Limited-Time Offer**
**Target**: Prospects who haven't booked demo
**Action**: Send limited-time offer
**Template**: Urgency-focused email with offer
**Time**: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

#### **Offer Strategy**
- **Discount**: 20% off first 3 months
- **Bonus**: Free implementation setup
- **Deadline**: End of month

### **Day 12: Friday - Break-up Email**
**Target**: Unresponsive prospects
**Action**: Send professional break-up email
**Template**: Break-up with future option
**Time**: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

#### **Break-up Strategy**
- **Professional**: No hard sell
- **Value**: Offer to stay in touch
- **Future**: Invite to reach out when ready

## Week 3: Demo Execution

### **Demo Preparation**
- **Technical Setup**: Demo environment ready
- **Content**: Tailored to each prospect
- **Follow-up**: Immediate post-demo materials

### **Demo Script**
1. **Introduction (2 min)**: Company understanding
2. **Problem Statement (3 min)**: Their specific challenges
3. **Solution Demo (8 min)**: Live platform demonstration
4. **ROI Analysis (2 min)**: Their potential savings
5. **Q&A (5 min)**: Technical and business questions

### **Post-Demo Follow-up**
- **Immediate**: Thank you email with recording
- **24 Hours**: Personalized follow-up with next steps
- **3 Days**: Trial invitation if interested

## Week 4: Trial Conversion

### **Trial Setup**

- **Onboarding**: Guided setup process
- **Support**: Dedicated trial support

### **Trial Success Metrics**
- **Activation**: 100% of trials activated within 24 hours

- **Conversion**: 30% of trials convert to paid

## Risk Mitigation

### **Low Response Rate**
- **Mitigation**: A/B test subject lines and content
- **Backup Plan**: Expand prospect list to 100 companies

### **Technical Issues**
- **Mitigation**: Test all demo environments beforehand
- **Backup Plan**: Have screenshots and video demos ready

### **Competitive Interference**
- **Mitigation**: Emphasize unique automated fixing capabilities
- **Backup Plan**: Focus on specific niches where we excel

## Campaign Optimization

### **A/B Testing Variables**
- **Subject Lines**: Technical vs benefit-focused
- **Email Content**: Short vs long format
- **Call-to-Action**: Demo vs trial vs consultation
- **Timing**: Morning vs afternoon sends

### **Optimization Schedule**
- **Weekly**: Review metrics and adjust strategy
- **Bi-weekly**: A/B test new variations
- **Monthly**: Evaluate overall campaign performance
