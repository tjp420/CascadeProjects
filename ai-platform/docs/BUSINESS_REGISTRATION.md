# Business Registration Plan for ReasonAI

## Entity Structure Options

### Option 1: LLC (Limited Liability Company)
**Recommended for early stage**

**Pros:**
- Limited personal liability protection
- Pass-through taxation (no corporate tax)
- Simple to set up and maintain
- Flexible management structure

**Cons:**
- Self-employment taxes on all income
- Limited ability to issue stock
- May be perceived as less professional than corporation

**Estimated Cost:** $300-800 (varies by state)

### Option 2: C-Corporation
**Recommended for growth stage/investors**

**Pros:**
- Can issue stock to investors
- Lower tax rate on retained earnings
- Perceived as more professional
- Easier to attract venture capital

**Cons:**
- Double taxation (corporate + personal)
- More complex compliance requirements
- Higher setup and maintenance costs

**Estimated Cost:** $800-2,000 (varies by state)

### Option 3: S-Corporation
**Hybrid approach**

**Pros:**
- Pass-through taxation like LLC
- Can issue stock
- Perceived as professional

**Cons:**
- Ownership restrictions (max 100 shareholders)
- More complex than LLC
- Limited to US citizens/residents

## Recommended Path: LLC → C-Corp Transition

### Phase 1: Start as LLC (Months 1-12)
- Lower initial costs and complexity
- Adequate liability protection
- Can convert to corporation later when needed
- Focus on product-market fit first

### Phase 2: Convert to C-Corp (Months 12-18)
- When raising significant funding
- When planning for IPO or acquisition
- When employee stock options become important

## Registration Steps

### Step 1: Choose State of Incorporation
**Recommended: Delaware or Wyoming**

**Delaware:**
- Business-friendly laws
- Strong court system (Court of Chancery)
- Investor preference
- Cost: ~$89 + franchise tax

**Wyoming:**
- Low costs
- Strong privacy protections
- No state income tax
- Cost: ~$100

**Home State:**
- Simpler local compliance
- May be sufficient initially
- Cost varies widely

### Step 2: Name Registration
**Business Name:** ReasonAI LLC

**Actions:**
1. Check name availability with Secretary of State
2. Reserve name if available
3. Register trade name if needed
4. Consider trademark protection

### Step 3: File Articles of Organization
**Required Information:**
- Company name and address
- Registered agent information
- Business purpose
- Management structure
- Duration (usually perpetual)

### Step 4: Obtain EIN (Employer Identification Number)
- Free from IRS
- Required for bank accounts and taxes
- Apply online at IRS.gov

### Step 5: Open Business Bank Account
- Keep business and personal finances separate
- Required for liability protection
- Choose business-friendly bank

### Step 6: Register for State Taxes
- Sales tax (if selling digital products)
- Employment taxes (when hiring)
- State income tax
- Local business licenses
