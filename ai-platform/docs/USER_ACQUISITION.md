# ReasonAI User Acquisition Strategy

## Target Audience Segments

### Primary Segment: Students (High School & College)
**Demographics:**
- Age: 16-24
- STEM-focused students
- Struggling with complex subjects
- Value stepwise learning

**Pain Points:**
- Confused by complex problem explanations
- Need help with homework/studying
- Want to understand "why" not just "what"
- Limited access to quality tutoring

**Acquisition Channels:**
- TikTok/Instagram educational content
- Student discount programs
- Campus ambassador program
- Partnerships with schools

### Secondary Segment: Parents (K-12)
**Demographics:**
- Age: 35-55
- College-educated parents
- Income: $75K+ household
- Value educational outcomes

**Pain Points:**
- Can't help with advanced homework
- Want affordable tutoring alternatives
- Concern about screen time quality
- Need progress tracking

**Acquisition Channels:**
- Facebook/Instagram parenting groups
- Educational blogs and newsletters
- School PTA partnerships
- Referral programs

### Tertiary Segment: Adult Learners
**Demographics:**
- Age: 25-45
- Career changers/upskillers
- Self-directed learners
- Professional certification seekers

**Pain Points:**
- Need flexible learning schedules
- Want practical application focus
- Require clear progression paths
- Budget-conscious

**Acquisition Channels:**
- LinkedIn professional content
- Reddit educational communities
- YouTube tutorial content
- Professional development platforms

## Marketing Funnel Strategy

### Top of Funnel: Awareness (1M+ impressions/month)
**Content Marketing:**
- "Stepwise Problem Solving" video series
- Educational blog posts (SEO optimized)
- Social media educational snippets
- Free problem-solving workshops

**Paid Acquisition:**
- Google Ads: "math homework help", "coding tutorials"
- Social media: Targeted to students/parents
- Educational platform partnerships

**Metrics:**
- CPM: <$5
- Click-through rate: >2%
- Cost per visitor: <$0.50

### Middle of Funnel: Consideration (50K+ leads/month)
**Lead Magnets:**
- Free stepwise problem analysis (3 problems)
- "Learn to Reason" mini-course
- Study guides and cheat sheets
- Progress tracking templates

**Nurturing:**
- Email education series
- Success stories and testimonials
- Interactive demo invitations
- Limited-time trial offers

**Metrics:**
- Lead conversion rate: >15%
- Email open rate: >25%
- Demo completion rate: >40%

### Bottom of Funnel: Conversion (5K+ trials/month)
**Conversion Tactics:**
- 7-day free trial with full access
- Student discount: 50% off first month
- Parent bundle: Family plans
- Money-back guarantee

**Urgency Triggers:**
- Exam preparation periods
- Limited-time promotions
- Holiday study periods
- Back-to-school campaigns

**Metrics:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion: >20%
- Customer acquisition cost: <$25
- Monthly recurring revenue growth: 25%+
