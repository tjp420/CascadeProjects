## Outreach Sequence Strategy

### **Week 1: Initial Contact**
- **Day 1**: Send primary outreach email
- **Day 3**: LinkedIn connection request
- **Day 5**: First follow-up with whitepaper

### **Week 2: Value Demonstration**
- **Day 8**: Second follow-up with case study
- **Day 10**: LinkedIn message after connection
- **Day 12**: Third follow-up with ROI calculator

### **Week 3: Urgency Creation**
- **Day 15**: Final follow-up with limited-time offer
- **Day 17**: LinkedIn message if no email response
- **Day 19**: Break-up email with future option

### **Week 4+: Nurturing**
- Add to newsletter for technical content
- Invite to webinars and events
- Share relevant case studies and insights

## Personalization Variables

### **Research Points for Each Prospect**
1. **Company Size**: Tailor ROI calculations
2. **Industry**: Use industry-specific templates
3. **Recent News**: Reference company achievements
4. **Tech Stack**: Mention relevant integrations
5. **Hiring Patterns**: Indicate team growth
6. **Pain Points**: Research common industry challenges

### **Customization Examples**
- **Hiring**: "I saw you're hiring senior developers"
- **Funding**: "Congratulations on your recent Series [X]"
- **Product Launch**: "Impressive launch of [product name]"
- **Tech Stack**: "I see you use [technology] - we integrate seamlessly"

## Metrics and Tracking

### **Key Performance Indicators**
- **Open Rate**: Target >30%
- **Response Rate**: Target >10%
- **Meeting Booked Rate**: Target >5%
- **Conversion Rate**: Target >20% from demo to trial

### **A/B Testing Variables**
- Subject lines
- Email length
- Call-to-action placement
- Personalization level
- Timing of sends

### **Follow-up Optimization**
- Best follow-up timing: 3-4 days after initial email
- Optimal follow-up count: 3-4 touches before break-up
- Best response times: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM

This comprehensive outreach system provides the foundation for systematic lead generation and customer acquisition.
