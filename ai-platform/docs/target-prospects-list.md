## Outreach Strategy

### **Phase 1: Initial Contact (Week 1)**
- **Target**: Top 20 prospects (highest priority)
- **Method**: Personalized email + LinkedIn connection
- **Message**: Technical value proposition focus
- **Goal**: 15% response rate

### **Phase 2: Follow-up (Week 2-3)**
- **Target**: All 50 prospects
- **Method**: Technical whitepaper + ROI calculator
- **Message**: Business impact focus
- **Goal**: 30% engagement rate

### **Phase 3: Demo Booking (Week 4-6)**
- **Target**: Responded prospects
- **Method**: Personalized demo invitation
- **Message**: Live demonstration of value
- **Goal**: 20% conversion to demo

### **Phase 4: Trial Conversion (Week 7-8)**
- **Target**: Demo attendees
- **Method**: Trial offer + onboarding
- **Message**: Risk-free trial period
- **Goal**: 30% conversion to paid trial

3. **Pain Points**: Industry-specific challenges
