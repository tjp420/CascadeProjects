❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with Cascade AI?
Getting started with Cascade AI is easy! Simply sign up for an account, complete the onboarding process, and you'll have access to all AI tools and features. We recommend starting with the "Getting Started" guide in the Help Center.
What AI models are supported?
Cascade AI supports a wide range of AI models including GPT, BERT, and custom models. You can upload your own models or use our pre-trained models for various tasks.
Is my data secure?
Yes, your data is secure. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
How do I integrate with my existing tools?
Cascade AI provides comprehensive API documentation and integration guides. You can integrate with your existing tools using our REST API or webhooks.
What support options are available?
We offer 24/7 email support, live chat support during business hours, and an extensive knowledge base in the Help Center. Premium users also get priority support.
How do I upgrade my plan?
You can upgrade your plan at any time from the Settings page. Simply select your desired plan and follow the upgrade process.
