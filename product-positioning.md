# Product Positioning Draft

## Target Personas

### 1. Enterprise Security Officer
- **Profile**: CISO/Security Lead at mid-to-large organizations (500+ employees)
- **Pain Point**: Managing complex security workflows across multiple tools and teams
- **Goal**: Centralized visibility and streamlined compliance reporting
- **Budget Authority**: Yes, directly controls security tool spending

### 2. Mid-Market DevOps Engineer
- **Profile**: DevOps/Platform Engineer at growth-stage companies (50-500 employees)
- **Pain Point**: Integrating security monitoring with deployment pipelines
- **Goal**: Automated security checks without slowing down development velocity
- **Budget Authority**: Recommender, approves within department budget

### 3. Security-First SaaS Founder
- **Profile**: Technical founder or VP Engineering building security-native products
- **Pain Point**: Building trustworthy security posture for customer assurance
- **Goal**: Demonstrate and communicate security capabilities to enterprise customers
- **Budget Authority**: Yes, makes purchasing decisions

---

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Unified Security Intelligence Platform
Consolidate security insights from multiple sources into a single dashboard, eliminating tool sprawl and reducing time spent switching between platforms.

### 2. Intelligent Risk Prioritization
AI-driven risk scoring and context-aware alerting focuses your team on what matters most, reducing alert fatigue and enabling faster incident response.

### 3. Compliance-First Architecture
Built-in compliance frameworks (SOC2, ISO 27001, GDPR) with automated audit trails and reporting, cutting compliance preparation time by 60%.
