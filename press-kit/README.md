## 📊 Media Outreach Strategy

### Tier 1: Technology Media (National)
- Target: Tier 1 tech publications
- Angle: AI in enterprise security, modernizing security practices
- Timeline: Send embargo materials 1 week before launch

### Tier 2: Industry Press & Analysts
- Target: Security, compliance, DevOps publications
- Angle: Enterprise features, compliance automation, supply chain security
- Timeline: Embargoed materials + early analyst briefings

### Tier 3: Developer & Community
- Target: Developer blogs, DevOps communities, security forums
- Angle: Easy deployment, CI/CD integration, developer-friendly
- Timeline: Post-launch community announcements

### Tier 4: B2B & Enterprise
- Target: Analyst firms, MSSP partners, enterprise consultants
- Angle: Partnership opportunities, ROI on security tooling
- Timeline: Direct outreach and follow-up calls

## 🔒 Embargo & Confidentiality

- All materials marked "EMBARGO" should not be published until embargo lift time
- Media embargoes typically 1-2 weeks before public launch
- Embargo times must be clearly communicated to all recipients
- Coordinate with marketing team on embargo lift schedule
