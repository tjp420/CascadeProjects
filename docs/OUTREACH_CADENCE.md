# Outreach Cadence & Templates (SimpleBeacon)

This document captures the 5-day outreach cadence and the exact templated messages to use when contacting startup CTOs.

## Cadence

Day 1: LinkedIn Connection Request (short) — keep it light.
Day 2: LinkedIn Direct Message — use the "Founders Audit" Hook.
Day 4: Follow-up Email — the "Deal Protector" context.
Day 5: Final breakaway message.

## LinkedIn Connection Request (Day 1)

Short, e.g.: "Congrats on the raise — I'd love to connect."

## LinkedIn Direct Message (Day 2)

Use the message generated by `tools/leads_manager.py message --channel linkedin` or the template below:

> Hey [First], congrats on growing the engineering team post-funding! Quick question: A major roadblock for B2B teams closing enterprise procurement is hidden, viral AGPL/copyleft licenses buried deep in sub-dependencies. I built a local parser that scans `package-lock.json` metadata for these legal blockers without accessing source code. I’m running a few confidential audits this week — would you be open to dropping a lockfile so I can run a quick check?

## Follow-up Email (Day 4)

Subject: Code supply chain risks at [Company Name]

Hi [First],

When B2B startups hit unexpected legal friction during enterprise acquisition, it's often due to an open-source package with a viral license (e.g., AGPL-3.0) buried in sub-dependencies. I reached out on LinkedIn about SimpleBeacon's localized Node license analyzer — it only parses dependency metadata, so your actual source stays private.

If you'd like an executive HTML compliance ledger to review with your team or board, reply with your project's lockfile and I'll send the dashboard within 24 hours.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## Final Breakaway Message (Day 5)

Short wrap-up, e.g.: "Wrapping up outreach this week — happy to run a free, confidential scan if you change your mind."

## Tracking & Follow-up

- Use `leads/leads_template.csv` to record progress.
- Use `tools/leads_manager.py export` to produce a markdown progress report for Slack or your CRM.

## Delivery Script (When They Say Yes)

1. Run: `python node_license_analyzer.py --lockfile ./their-lockfile.json`
2. Send the generated `license-compliance-report.html`.
3. Attach `.github/pull_request_template/compliance-remediation.md` and `docs/REMEDIATION-PLAYBOOK.md`.
4. Offer to drop the `.github/workflows/license-gate.yml` into their repo for $99/month continuous protection.
