fix: eliminate 48 export collision warnings from simplebeacon barrel loader

The barrel loader in packages/simplebeacon-cli/src/index.js flattens ~50 module
exports into one namespace. Seven modules re-exported utility functions that
were already exported by dedicated utility modules, causing 48
SimplebeaconExportCollision warnings on every server/CLI startup.

Root cause: modules like reporters/audit-report.js and lib/ai-problem-analyzer-suite.js
defined their own local copies of 30+ utility functions (isBlank, isEmpty, capitalize,
sleep, delay, hash, etc.) and re-exported them, colliding with the canonical
utils/string, utils/object, utils/functional, and utils/async modules.

Fix: removed utility re-exports from 7 secondary modules. Enriched the canonical
utility modules with functions that were only defined in secondary modules:
- utils/string.js: added capitalize, pluralize, truncate
- utils/functional.js: added memoize

Also removed lib/ai-problem-analyzer-suite from the Simplebeacon.utils namespace
(it's an analyzer module, not a utility module).

Updated 2 tests to reflect new canonical locations:
- tests/scan.test.js: import formatBytes from lib/format-utils
- tests/index.test.js: check Simplebeacon.report.compileAuditReportMarkdown
  instead of capitalize/pluralize/truncate (now in Simplebeacon.utils)

from lib/format-utils instead of ../scan.
